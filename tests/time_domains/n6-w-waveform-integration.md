# N6-W controlled machine-history / native-waveform binding

Run `python3 tools/test_n6_w.py --backend both --output .local/n6-w/qualified` from the repository root. Use `--sanitizer` for the W0 native generator/replay ASan+UBSan profile; the i286 semantic owner may also be selected with `--i286-binary .local/n6-c/build-i286-asan/sdlnp2kai_sdl2`. The product binaries must have `NP2_OPNA_TIMER_MACHINE_TIME` and `NP2_OPNA_CSM_HISTORY` enabled and the existing fake machine-time fixture. The only wall-clock use is GDB process orchestration. Neither product code nor a public format/API is changed.

The semantic source is the actual N6-C PC-9801-86 (`0x04`) native OPNA board/timer/I/O/PIC path driven by controlled fake machine time. `tests/time_domains/n6_w_semantic_binding.py` captures each admitted `OPNA_CSM_HISTORY_RECORD`, finalized prefix count, epoch, sequence, logical expiry deadline, service time, Timer A ordinal, CSM class, and off/on subindices. It checks the expected rational schedule, including a ten-expiry single-service case, a timer period rewrite, stop/restart gap, and reset into a new epoch. Same-time guest key and high/low frequency writes are observed after the CSM prefix is finalized. Breakpoints assert no `sound_sync`, `opngen_csm`, or `opngen_getpcm` calls **inside machine-time service**. The existing N6-C capacity-rejection fixture establishes unchanged state and no accepted partial history; this bridge publishes no worker plan or PCM on that rejection.

The waveform-plan source is separate: the W0 recorder runs the legacy native generator under its fixed 44,100 Hz, stereo signed 32-bit producer profile. Its schema-1 records supply exact epoch-local sample frontiers, generation call IDs, start frontiers, requested/actual frame counts, call origin, reset, register/key ordering, and finalization. The private W0 schema is unchanged. Host fixture 13 adds two CSM pairs at a single producer frontier; W0 now has 12 positive cases and retains 16 negative controls. The W0 50,000-record/200,000-frame bounds and the N6-C 256-record history bound are test capacities, not product debt limits.

`tools/test_n6_w.py` checks fixture identity, N6-C record class and exact finalized sequence prefix, expected logical deadlines, epoch mapping (machine epochs 2/3 to W0 epochs 1/2 in these cold-start fixtures), off→on sub-order, and one-to-one CSM slot count. Each mapped pair retains the W0 parent sequence and off/on edge order. It checks same-frontier key/frequency ordering and the batched-service case without collapsing distinct CSM records. The controlled source plan supplies each CSM slot's **producer frontier and call partition**; the binder materializes the CSM pair and edges into those slots from admitted N6-C records. Materialized bytes must equal the separately recorded W0 source plan. The matching rule never compares machine nanoseconds to sample indices and never computes a sample position from a deadline.

The W0 worker replays the bound binary plan in immediate and delayed modes. Delayed mode validates the complete plan, copies it to worker-owned storage, clears the source record buffer, and applies only finalized prefixes. Source recording and both replays are separate processes. For each case, the report records semantic-history and bound-plan SHA-256, event/CSM/generation-call/epoch counts, total frames and PCM bytes, PCM SHA-256, and the W0 explicit-scalar final renderer fingerprint. The runner compares source, immediate, and delayed producer PCM byte for byte, final-state bytes, and consumed plan bytes. The report lives under the requested ignored `.local/` directory.

Positive fixtures P0–P9 and P11 map to W0 cases 1,2,3,3,4,5,13,3,3,6,10. P10 records both known quiet-tail plans (W0 8/9) against the same one-CSM semantic scenario. Their 11,000 total frames and semantic operations agree, but different generation-call partitions produce distinct PCM beginning at frame 10,000. The W0 10/11 active-tone alternative has the same semantic operations and final PCM/fingerprint with a different partition only in that bounded fixture. It is not a general repartition rule.

The integrated negative matrix has 24 entries: missing/extra/swapped CSM; off/on inversion; CSM/guest-key and frequency-write reversal; wrong/stale epoch and wrong sequence even with equal service time; ±1 frontier; generation split/merge/reorder/count corruption; early or incomplete finalization; retrospective semantic deadline; plan without matching semantics and semantics without a plan slot; and 1.5×/1.7× semantic deadline stretch. Each reports the first rejected semantic record or plan entry where practical. Some corruptions do not affect PCM; they still fail before waveform replay. W0's own 16 negative controls remain independent.

These results prove exact **controlled** replay under an independently supplied qualified plan. They do not qualify a general machine-time-to-sample mapping, physical YM2608 sub-sample timing, live SDL CSM waveform playback, PCM86, FMGEN, ADPCM, Speak Board, P4 worker/service behavior, or realtime deadlines. Existing FMP traces support event identity and producer-duration trends only; their PCM is not a byte oracle. The next mission may audit the P4 owner-service/delayed-worker bridge, but this test makes no P4 protocol or product change.
